Born from the need for simpler compliance

JDN Corporate Tech India LLP was established in 2018 in New Delhi by a team of regulatory experts, legal professionals and technology enthusiasts. They saw a clear gap: Indian businesses, particularly MSMEs and startups, were struggling with the complexity, time and cost of obtaining government licences and regulatory approvals.

We built JDN as a one-stop compliance partner, combining deep regulatory knowledge with a client-first approach to make government licensing accessible, fast and transparent for every business, regardless of size or industry.

Today, we serve clients across India from our base at ITL Twin Tower, Netaji Subhash Place, New Delhi. Our work covers LMPC, EPR and BIS certifications, GST registration, trademark filings and full-stack digital services.

Our mission

Making compliance simple for every Indian business.

We believe that navigating government regulations should never be a barrier to doing business. Our mission is to simplify, accelerate and demystify the licensing process, so you can focus on what you do best: growing your business.

Key milestones

From a small team of compliance experts to one of Delhi's trusted regulatory consultancies, our journey has been defined by steady growth and client success.

2018: Foundation

JDN Corporate Tech India LLP established. Founded in New Delhi with a core team of 4 compliance and legal experts, focusing first on LMPC and import-export licensing.

2019: Expansion

Added BIS, EPR and FSSAI services. We expanded to cover Bureau of Indian Standards certifications, EPR authorisations and food safety compliance.

2021: Digital launch

Full digital consultancy platform. We introduced online consultations, digital document management and remote compliance support, serving clients across all 28 states.

2023: Web and tech services

2026: Today
